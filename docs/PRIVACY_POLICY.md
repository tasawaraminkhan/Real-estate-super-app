# PropVerse Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 4 November 2026

PropVerse (“we”, “the app”) is a Pakistan-focused real estate marketplace application.

## Data we collect
- **Account:** phone number (for OTP sign-in), optional name and CNIC for KYC when you submit it.
- **Listings & leads:** property details, prices, and lead notes you enter.
- **Usage:** basic crash and performance diagnostics when enabled in production builds.

## How we use data
- Authenticate you and secure your account.
- Show and manage listings, search, cost estimates, and lead tools.
- Prevent fraud and abuse (including risk scoring on listing submit).

## What we do **not** do
- We do not sell personal data.
- We do not use your contacts, SMS, or call logs.
- Demo builds may use offline sample data and do not send real payments.

## Third parties
- SMS OTP and payment partners (JazzCash / Easypaisa) only when production keys are configured.
- Cloud storage for listing photos when media upload is enabled.

## Your choices
- Request account deletion by contacting support@propverse.app (or the publisher email on Play Store).
- Log out from Profile at any time.
