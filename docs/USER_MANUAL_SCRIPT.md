Welcome to the complete user guide for PropVerse — Pakistan’s real estate super app.

This video will teach you every feature step by step. After watching, you will be able to use PropVerse yourself and teach others.

Chapter one. What is PropVerse?

PropVerse helps you buy, sell, and rent property in Pakistan. You can also estimate construction cost, check legal and fraud signals, manage leads, and explore society maps. The app uses local units such as Marla and Kanal, and supports major Pakistani cities.

Chapter two. Opening the app and signing in.

Install PropVerse on your Android phone. Open the app. You will see Sign in with mobile. Enter your Pakistani mobile number, for example starting with zero three. Tap Send OTP.

In the demo version, any valid number works. Enter the code one two three four five six, then tap Verify and continue. You are now signed in.

Chapter three. Home screen overview.

At the top you see PropVerse and a Profile link. Below is a search box. Type a city, society, or DHA name to filter listings.

Next are category chips: All, Buy, Rent, and Commercial. Tap any chip to filter. Below that are price filters such as Any price, under fifty Lac, fifty to one hundred Lac, one to three Crore, and three Crore plus.

Then you see Quick Tools. These shortcuts open Legal and Fraud Check, Escrow Safe Deal, Cost and Materials, Fractional Invest, and the Labour Market. We will open each later.

Under Quick Tools is the listings list. Each card shows a photo, price in green, title, location, area, bedrooms and bathrooms when available, plus Call and WhatsApp buttons.

Chapter four. Searching and filtering properties.

Tap Buy to see only sale listings. Tap Rent for rental properties. Tap Commercial for shops and offices. Combine with a price chip. If nothing matches, you will see No listings match your filters. Tap All and Any price to reset.

Use the search box for city or society names such as Lahore, Bahria, or DHA.

Chapter five. Opening a listing and contacting the seller.

Tap any property card. You open the detail screen with a large photo, price, title, area, city, purpose badge such as Sale, and short about text.

From detail you can Chat, Save to favourites, Call masked, or WhatsApp. In demo mode, Call and WhatsApp show a safe message because sample numbers are not real. In production they open a verified or masked contact.

Tap Chat to message about the property. Type your message and Send. Use Back to return.

Chapter six. Quick Tools — Legal and Fraud Check.

From Home, tap Legal and Fraud Check. Enter a token or property reference if you have one, or society details. Tap Run fraud scan. The demo shows a sample risk style result. Always still verify fard and registry with official sources before any payment.

Chapter seven. Escrow Safe Deal.

Open Escrow Safe Deal from Quick Tools. Choose Buyer or Owner role. Enter a deal token if provided. Review milestone style steps. This is a demonstration of how a safe deal flow can work. No real money moves in the demo. Do not treat it as a live bank escrow until the product is linked to licensed partners.

Chapter eight. Construction Cost calculator — full walkthrough.

Tap the Cost tab at the bottom, or open Cost and Materials from Quick Tools. On Construction Cost, choose area size with the number chips or type a custom number. Select Marla, Kanal, or Square feet.

Enter number of floors. Choose construction type: Grey Structure, Turnkey, or Semi Finished. Choose quality: Economy, Standard, Premium, or Luxury.

Select a city. You can search or tap Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, and other cities, or add Custom. Tap Calculate Cost.

You will see total estimate in Lac or Crore, a range, cost per square foot, and a breakdown for materials, finishing, and labour. These are sample twenty twenty five style rates. Always confirm with local contractors.

Chapter nine. Society Map.

Tap the Map tab. You see Society Map with layer chips: NOC only, Commercial, High ROI, and Under construction. Tap a layer to turn it on or off. Plots appear as coloured squares: green available, yellow reserved, red sold, grey blocked.

Tap a plot to see plot number, status, area, price if available, and whether NOC is approved. Use this as an inventory style view of a master plan demo.

Chapter ten. Create a listing — Sell tab.

Tap Sell at the bottom. You start Create Listing step one of four — Type. Choose purpose: Sale, Rent, or Short Stay. Choose property type: Plot, House, Flat, Shop, Office, Villa, Farmhouse, or Warehouse. Tap Next Location.

Step two — Location. Select city. You can add Custom. Optionally type society or project, title, and address. Tap Next Price and size.

Step three — enter price. You can use Lac, Crore, or full amount style inputs depending on the screen. Enter area value and unit. Add bedrooms and bathrooms if relevant. Continue to Review.

Step four — Review your details, then Submit listing. In demo, the listing is accepted locally or against the API when connected.

Chapter eleven. Leads for agents.

Tap the Leads tab. You see Lead Management System. Search by name or property. Filter by All, New, Contacted, Visit, or Negotiation. Each lead shows name, property, status badge, Call and WhatsApp. Use this to track buyers interested in your listings.

Chapter twelve. Fractional Invest and Labour Board.

From Quick Tools open Fractional Invest to see sample investment pools and Invest buttons. This is educational demo content only.

Open Labour Market to see sample verified style contractors with rates and Call actions. Use it to understand how a labour board can work. Verify any real worker independently.

Chapter thirteen. Profile, KYC, favourites, and plans.

Tap Profile on the home header. Edit full name. Choose role: Buyer, Owner, or Agent. Enter CNIC in the format five digits, dash, seven digits, dash, one digit, then Submit KYC. Status moves to Pending in demo.

Open My favourites to see saved properties. Open Subscription plans to view plan options. Tap Log out when you want to leave. You return to the sign in screen.

Chapter fourteen. Safety tips for every user.

Never pay token or full amount without verifying ownership documents. Use Legal and Fraud Check as a helper, not a final legal opinion. Prefer masked call and chat inside the app when available. Construction estimates are guides only. Escrow and investment features are demo until officially enabled with partners.

Chapter fifteen. You are ready.

You now know how to sign in, search and filter, open listings, chat, calculate construction cost, use the society map, create a listing, manage leads, open quick tools, and manage your profile.

Open PropVerse, practice each tab once, and you can teach family or colleagues the same steps. Thank you for watching the PropVerse complete user guide.
